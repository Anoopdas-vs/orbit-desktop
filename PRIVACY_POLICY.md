# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how "Orbit" (also known as "Janki Assistant") handles your data. Our primary commitment is to your privacy, which is why Orbit is built as a **local-first** application.

## 1. Local-First Approach
Orbit is designed to keep your data on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data, chat history, or usage data on our company servers. There is no central database of user information maintained by us.

## 2. Data Stored Locally
The following data is generated and stored locally on your device to enable the Software's features:
- **Voice Commands:** Processed locally or through APIs you explicitly configure.
- **Audit Logs:** Activity logs are kept on your local machine for troubleshooting and transparency.
- **Trade Journal:** Financial and trading data you log is stored entirely in your local database.

## 3. Network Communications
While the app operates locally, it makes specific external network requests based on the features you use. **These communications happen directly between your device and the third-party service; we do not intercept or proxy these requests.**
- **Ollama (Local):** If you configure a local Ollama instance, the app communicates with it over your local network.
- **Binance Public API:** When using the trading module, the app fetches market data and executes trades directly via the Binance API using your provided API keys.
- **YouTube:** If using multimedia features, the app may retrieve metadata or content directly from YouTube.

## 4. Third-Party API Keys and Security
- **macOS Keychain:** Your sensitive credentials, such as API keys (e.g., for Binance or other services), are stored securely in your system's macOS Keychain. The app only accesses them when necessary to authenticate requests you initiate.

## 5. Microphone Access
Orbit requires microphone access to function with voice commands. Audio data is captured only when you actively trigger voice features and is processed locally or sent directly to the speech-to-text API you configure. We do not listen to or record your background conversations.

## 6. No Sale of Data
Because we do not collect your personal data on our servers, we have no data to sell, share, or rent to third parties.

## 7. Data Deletion and User Rights
You have full control over your data.
- **Right to Deletion:** You can delete all your data at any time using the app's built-in reset feature, which purges local databases, logs, and stored preferences.
- **GDPR and DPDPA Compliance:** As we do not process personal data on our servers, your use of the app naturally complies with the minimization principles of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA). Your data stays with you.
